# r35: evidence-based handling of disputed AI validation

This downstream correction retains upstream
`410951e74fd3e6b7a763cf49757935b9a34d3f31` and all r34 behavior, including
advisory unassigned form actions. It adds one canonical AI evidence rule and
routes the gate, call-budget, delivery and agent instructions through it.

An empty, links-only or wrong-unit response is unverified. One documented
recovery call is allowed within the existing per-validator budget; it neither
adds an attempt nor permits repeated requests for green text. Substantive
unsupported claims remain open findings rather than unusable responses.

The exact validation unit/context, raw responses, original findings, evidence,
decision authority and hashes remain available for review and freshness checks.
Mechanically proven contradictions may be resolved under the policy; ambiguous
business meaning and real risk acceptance require an authorized decision.
Adjudicated acceptance is reported separately from a clean tool response.
Confirmed defects still need correction and fresh applicable evidence. Syntax,
metadata, impact, runtime and host ITL gates remain independently enforced.

The existing ordered-validator checks and real installation tests for all ten
clients pass, including manifest ownership and preservation of the complete
policy body. These checks establish routing/distribution, not correctness of an
agent's future rebuttal. The retained branch11 responses still require exact-unit
assessment. Fork Full qualification, workflow compatibility promotion, normal
delivery and installed acceptance remain distinct requirements.

## Scope

This downstream-only policy release fixes quick-fix classification in the
compact root router. It applies the unchanged upstream promotion triggers
without treating every correction of existing behavior as full-cycle. An
otherwise eligible internal BSL fix may remain quick-fix when it preserves
public contracts and avoids the upstream promotion triggers.

The ITL completion invariant is unchanged: relevant Vanessa coverage and a
fresh successful `/itl-check` after the last change remain mandatory before an
agent reports an installed-project configuration or extension change ready or
done.
